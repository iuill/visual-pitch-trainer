Commit: 440300901dfe9275fd84e0b7763af1f8443ae62e
Solo Violin / Arco Vib (p), Flute / susNV (v1).
The violin recording retains slight pitch variation. Low notes outside the
recorded range are generated by transposing the nearest recording.

Harmonium: "Harmonium Samples - All Keys and Drones" by Donya Quick
(donyaquick), recorded in the Euterpea Studio at Yale University.
https://freesound.org/people/donyaquick/sounds/330410/
Source format: Freesound HQ MP3 preview of the original 96 kHz WAV.
https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/330/330410_1089898-hq.mp3
Individual sustain excerpts were extracted; C6 is above the recorded D5 limit
and is produced by playback-rate transposition.

Modifications: downmix/resample to mono PCM16 at 44.1 kHz; extract stable
sustain segments; crossfade loop boundaries; remove DC; normalize peak;
measure loop pitch for playback-rate calibration. Recorded attacks and
releases are replaced by a short gain envelope in the player. These are
adapted practice sounds, not complete reproductions of the instruments.

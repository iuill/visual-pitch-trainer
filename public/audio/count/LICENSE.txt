Count audio (male and female voices, long and short versions)

These files contain single English counting words synthesized using the stock
eSpeak NG en-us and en-us+f3 formant voices. eSpeak's maintainer states that
the program's GPL does not apply to generated speech WAV output.

eSpeak NG is the authoring tool, licensed GPL-3.0-or-later. The application
ships only the generated WAV files. See sources.json for the generation
parameters and SHA-256 checksums.
